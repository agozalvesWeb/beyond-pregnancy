Fueling an active body while breastfeeding

Breastfeeding and exercise are generally compatible for most healthy people. Athletic mothers may need to plan for comfort, time, hydration and sufficient energy. Needs vary with milk production, training load, body size, health and infant feeding patterns.

Women's sports medicineEducational guide

Comfort and logistics

  • A supportive, comfortable sports bra may help.
  • Feeding or pumping before exercise may improve comfort for some people.
  • Plan around the baby's needs without treating a schedule as a strict rule.

Fuel for recovery and performance

  • Avoid aggressive calorie restriction or rapid weight-loss goals.
  • Include regular meals and snacks with carbohydrates, protein and dietary variety.
  • Hydration needs vary; drink to thirst and consider longer or hot sessions.

When to seek support

  • A registered dietitian with sports and lactation experience can help with individual needs.
  • Seek lactation support for persistent nipple pain, breast inflammation, fever or feeding concerns.
Important: Do not use this page to make decisions about infant feeding or medication. Consult a qualified lactation professional or healthcare clinician for individual concerns.
