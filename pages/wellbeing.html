The mental side of athletic motherhood

The transition to parenthood can affect identity, confidence, relationships, sleep and expectations about performance. Athletes may feel pressure to return quickly. Recovery includes emotional wellbeing and practical support—not only physical training.

Women's sports medicineEducational guide

Make room for changing identity

  • It is normal for goals and priorities to change during pregnancy and parenthood.
  • Avoid comparing your timeline with another athlete's experience.
  • Rest and asking for help are part of a sustainable plan.

Sleep, stress and support

  • Sleep disruption can influence mood, coordination, recovery and training tolerance.
  • Share care responsibilities where possible and accept practical help.
  • Build a support network that respects both your athletic and parenting needs.

When to seek help

  • Persistent sadness, anxiety, panic, intrusive thoughts or difficulty functioning deserve professional attention.
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or your baby require immediate help from emergency services or a healthcare professional.
Important: Postpartum depression and anxiety are treatable health conditions. Seeking support is a sign of care, not weakness.
