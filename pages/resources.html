Quick reference
When to seek medical help
Stop exercise and seek urgent medical care for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, severe headache or visual changes, or heavy bleeding.
Contact a qualified clinician for persistent pain, pelvic heaviness, urinary or bowel leakage, worsening symptoms, wound concerns or distressing mood changes.
